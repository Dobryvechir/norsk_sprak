Norsk Audio: listen, read and practise Norwegian

Norwegian audio with the text shown sentence by sentence, books with audio chapters, AI transcription of your own recordings and a chat for your learning group. On Android, iPhone and in the browser.

Open the web app → Create an account

What you can do

🎧Audio with text

Listen to Norwegian recordings while the current sentence is highlighted. Tap a sentence to jump to it, repeat each sentence up to 20 times and set the speed from 0.5× to 1.5×.

✨AI transcription

Upload your own audio and the AI turns it into Norwegian text, split into timed sentences, ready for listening practice. Included in the Pro plan.

📚Books

A library of books with audio for every chapter, including the Bible in Norwegian, with the text following the audio.

🔤Words and dictionaries

Look up the meaning of Norwegian words while you read and listen.

⬇Offline listening

Save chapters on your phone and keep listening without an internet connection.

💬Group chat

Every learning group has its own chat with subgroups and shared links. Family groups can also use "Find location" between members.

📄Documents and media

Keep PDFs and other documents on your device, record audio, take photos and sync everything with your own Google Drive.

🌍In your language

The app speaks 17 languages, including Norwegian (bokmål and nynorsk), English, Ukrainian, Polish and Russian.
