3. Payment card data

Payments are handled by Vipps MobilePay AS (Vipps) and Stripe (Apple Pay and Google Pay). Your card number goes directly to them and never reaches our server. They process your payment data under their own privacy policies (Vipps MobilePay, Stripe). The payment page loads Stripe's script, which may set cookies that Stripe uses to prevent fraud.

4. Who processes data for us

  • Hosting: Host Hatch, in Norway, where the server and the data are stored.
  • Email: messages such as the email-confirmation link are sent from our own mail server at movna-hatka.com (from noreply@movna-hatka.com), on the same hosting in Norway. No outside email service is used.
  • OpenAI (USA): audio is sent to OpenAI for automatic transcription when a group uses the AI services. OpenAI does not use data sent through its API to train its models.
  • Google: if you connect Google Drive in the app, the app reads and saves files in your own Drive with your permission. Those files stay in your Google account.

Where data is transferred outside the EEA (for example to the USA), the transfer is based on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. How long we keep data

  • An administrator approves or rejects every sign-up request within 10 business days. A rejected request is deleted at once; an approved one becomes your account.
  • Your account data is kept while you have an account, and deleted when the account is deleted.
  • Chat messages are deleted automatically after the number of days set for the group.
  • Payment records are kept for 5 years after the end of the financial year, as required by the Norwegian Bookkeeping Act (bokføringsloven).

6. Your rights

You may ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing and data portability, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to zhenya.dbv@gmail.com; we answer within one month. If you think we handle your data unlawfully, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).

7. Security

Passwords are stored only as secure hashes, connections use HTTPS, and access to data is limited to your groups and to administrators who need it.
